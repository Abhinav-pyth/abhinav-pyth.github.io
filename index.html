The biggest entertainment launch on record?

Grand Theft Auto VI is the first mainline entry since 2013's Grand Theft Auto V — a gap of thirteen years. Rockstar has spent the era between releases rebuilding its technology stack from the ground up: a denser world simulation, a new generation of character animation and performance capture, and a renderer built around modern lighting.

The story follows Lucia and her partner Jason — a Bonnie-and-Clyde pairing moving through the criminal underworld of Leonida. Trailer footage has shown prison time, a probation-style release, nightclub robberies, swamp-town deals and a state that feels alive whether or not you are looking at it.

Case file 009 · Verification

Facts, reports & noise

A quick triage of what belongs in which bucket as of November 2026.

Confirmed by Rockstar

  • Release date: November 19, 2026.
  • Platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • Setting: the state of Leonida, including Vice City.
  • Protagonists: Lucia and Jason, playable as a pair.
  • Named regions: Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Fort Baxter, Grassrivers, Leonida Keys, Mount Kalaga.
  • Two official trailers released (2023 and 2025).
  • A new online component is planned for the future.

Credibly reported

  • Development began around 2014–2015, with full production from 2018 onward.
  • Work spread across multiple Rockstar studios worldwide.
  • Crunch practices were reduced following the 2018–2019 scrutiny.
  • Licensed music deal involves a very large station roster.
  • Take-Two expects it to be the largest entertainment launch in history.
  • Regional price equivalents compiled from storefront listings (see pricing section).

Rumour / unverified

  • Map-size numbers quoted in “square miles” — no official figure exists.
  • Multiple city hubs beyond Vice City as fully separate metros.
  • 60fps performance modes on base consoles.
  • Cloud-streaming or handheld versions at launch.
  • Official INR pricing for India — the ₹ figures here are conversions, not a Rockstar price list.
  • Any “leaked” build, ISO or download link — these are scams or malware.
